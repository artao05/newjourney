## Provenance note

The Expedition analysis in `docs/01-expedition-analysis/` is derived **entirely** from the
publicly downloadable official manual and vendor website. No reverse engineering of
binaries, no decompilation, no licence circumvention. Quotations are short, attributed
excerpts used for interoperability analysis and comparative review.

The routing algorithms in `docs/03-algorithms/` are written from published literature and
from Expedition's own public documentation of its behaviour. **No GPL source was consulted
while writing them** — see
[02-data-sources/licensing-matrix.md §5](02-data-sources/licensing-matrix.md#5-gpl-contamination--the-real-risk).
